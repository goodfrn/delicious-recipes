ingredients:
- "8 cups pretzels, any shape"
- "6 tablespoons cinnamon butter, softened or melted"
- "2 tablespoons ranch seasoning mix"
- "1 tablespoon smoked paprika"
- "1 teaspoon garlic salt"
instructions:
- "Preheat oven to 280°F. Low temp coax crispiness without burning butter or spices."
- "Slice softened cinnamon butter evenly into chunks. Microwave in 10 second bursts, stir after each, just until fully melted not bubbling. Avoid overheating butter: acrid burnt fat kills flavor."
- "In a large bowl, toss pretzels with melted butter. Get every piece coated but no pools of butter. Toss in ranch seasoning, smoked paprika and garlic salt. Mix thoroughly. Break clumps with hands if needed."
- "Scatter pretzels evenly on a rimmed baking sheet lined with parchment or silpat for easy cleanup. Single layer only. Overlapping pretzels steam instead of toast."
- "Bake 25 minutes, stirring once halfway through. Watch: edges should darken from pale tan to golden brown. Smell shifts from sweet butter to toasted spice. Pretzels crack audibly when pinched."
- "Remove from oven, let cool completely on sheet. Butter firms back up; prevents sticky mess. Taste test crispness. If soft, pop back in 3-5 minutes and watch closely."
- "Serve at room temp. Can store airtight for a few days. To revive crispness, toss in dry skillet over medium heat briefly. Smoke paprika swaps smoky heat for smoky warmth. Cinnamon butter adds a touch of sweet earthiness instead of malt richness."
- "Substitutions: use salted butter plus teaspoon sugar if cinnamon butter unavailable. Ranch seasoning can be homemade with dried dill, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper. Garlic salt replaced by regular garlic powder plus salt to taste. Adjust seasonings toward preference, toss and test flavor before baking."
