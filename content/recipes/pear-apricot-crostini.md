ingredients:
- "8 slices baguette, cut 1/4 inch thick"
- "3 tablespoons olive oil"
- "1/2 cup chopped red onion"
- "2 medium pears, diced small"
- "1/3 cup apricot preserves"
- "2 tablespoons honey"
- "1/4 cup water"
- "2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar"
- "1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt"
- "1 cinnamon stick"
- "1 teaspoon fresh chopped rosemary"
- "1/3 cup toasted pecans, chopped"
- "1/3 cup crumbled blue cheese"
instructions:
- "Set oven to 310 degrees Fahrenheit. Arrange bread slices flat on large sheet. Bake 8–12 minutes per side. Look for light golden edges and firm, dry texture. No char, no soft middle. Cool on wire rack."
- "Heat olive oil in heavy skillet over medium-high flame until shimmering. Toss in red onion pieces. Stir as they soften and turn translucent—about 4 minutes. Watch carefully so they don’t brown or burn; burned onions ruin balance."
- "Add diced pears, apricot preserves, honey, water, apple cider vinegar, and salt. Stir to combine fully. Cinnamon stick goes in now for slow aromatic release."
- "Lower heat to medium-low. Let mixture bubble gently—not full boil, but steady simmer. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. After about 18 minutes, most liquid should evaporate. Mixture thickens, looks glossy and jam-like with sticky consistency. Wrinkle test: run spoon through—leave a clear path, sides set."
- "Remove pot from heat; fish out cinnamon stick. Stir in fresh rosemary, still hot enough to release oils but not wilt herb."
- "Spoon generous dollops onto toasted bread slices. Top with chopped pecans then crumble blue cheese over each. Serve immediately before crostini sog."
- "Pro tip: If fresh pears are too soft or watery, pat dry or swap with firm Asian pear. Blue cheese too sharp? Try goat cheese for milder tang. Honey darkens the compote flavor compared to agave; adjust quantity to taste."
- "Toasting bread evenly critical: slice thickness must be consistent. Oven temp steady around 310 to dry without over-baking. One side toasted enough flip; if unsure, tap crust—should sound hollow and feel brittle."
- "Simmer mixture low—not boiling—to avoid burning sugars. Stir to avoid crust forming on pan bottom. Cinnamon stick not ground to prevent gritty bits and bitterness. Fresh rosemary added last keeps bright fragrance without overpowering."
