ingredients:
- "3 large chicken breasts, cut into 1½ to 2-inch cubes"
- "½ cup extra virgin olive oil"
- "4 garlic cloves, finely minced"
- "1 teaspoon dried oregano or substitute with thyme"
- "1 teaspoon smoked paprika instead of chili powder"
- "1 teaspoon sea salt"
- "½ teaspoon black pepper freshly ground"
- "Wooden skewers soaked 45 minutes or metal skewers"
- "For aioli:"
- "½ cup mayonnaise (use greek yogurt for lighter option)"
- "1 garlic clove minced"
- "2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice"
- "1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil instead of parsley"
- "Pinch crushed red pepper flakes"
- "Fresh grated Parmesan cheese for garnish"
- "Fresh basil leaves for garnish"
instructions:
- "===Prep and marinate==="
- "Soak wooden skewers in warm water minimum 45 minutes to keep tips from burning. Metal skewers skip this. Cut chicken breasts into uniform 1½ to 2-inch cubes for even cooking. Too small makes them dry and stringy; too big slows cooking and risks raw centers."
- "Mix chicken cubes in a bowl with olive oil, minced garlic, sea salt, dried oregano or thyme, smoked paprika, and pepper. Toss to coat fully so all pieces get flavor. Let marinate about 20 minutes at room temperature. Avoid longer than 30 minutes—acid and salt start breaking down proteins, changing texture to mushy."
- "===Make aioli==="
- "While chicken marinates, whisk mayonnaise (or Greek yogurt if lighter), fresh lemon juice, garlic, chopped basil, and red pepper flakes in a small bowl. Balance lemon—too much turns it sour, not enough leaves it flat. Chill aioli covered until serving to let flavors meld. Basil swap works well here for a fresh summery twist."
- "===Threading and cooking==="
- "Thread marinated chicken on skewers, 6-7 pieces each. Crowding makes them steam, not brown. Leave gaps to allow hot air or grill heat to surround pieces. If skewers are long and won’t fit air fryer, trim them—or grill outside."
- "Air fryer cooks quickly, 400°F is target temp. Preheat if your model needs it, then air fry 12 minutes total turning once at about halfway mark when edges caramelize and juices run clear. Look for golden brown slightly crisp surface. Check with instant read thermometer—165°F is safe."
- "Oven option: place skewers on parchment-lined pan at 460°F. Bake 12 to 17 minutes flipping once. Visual clues again—sizzling sound, golden crust, juices clear when pricked."
- "Grilling calls for medium-high heat. Grill 7-9 minutes per side. Look for grill marks, avoid burning by moving skewers around spots where flames flare. Let juices rest momentarily before basting."
- "===Finishing==="
- "Right off heat, brush aioli all over chicken. Don’t wait or sauce will just slide off cold surface. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan and scatter torn fresh basil or chili flakes for color and heat. Serve immediately while warm and saucy."
- "Choose sides that soak extra juice: fluffy rice, tangy orzo salad, roasted squash, or grilled zucchini. Vegetables with bite contrast soft chicken."
- "===Troubleshooting==="
- "If chicken sticks when threading, oil skewers lightly. Dry chicken too long? Adds to toughness; keep covered till ready. If aioli tastes flat, crush garlic finer or add pinch salt."
- "If using frozen chicken, thaw fully and pat dry. Moisture impedes searing and can mute seasoning punch. Avoid thick pieces on skewers—uneven cooking is most common error here."
