ingredients:
- "3 tablespoons olive oil, or avocado oil"
- "2 medium yellow onions, diced"
- "4 cloves garlic, finely minced"
- "5 cups peeled butternut squash cubes (about 1 1/4 pounds)"
- "5 cups vegetable broth, homemade or store-bought"
- "1 teaspoon sea salt, plus more to taste"
- "1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, divided"
- "1/2 teaspoon dried thyme"
- "1/2 teaspoon dried rosemary, crushed"
- "1/4 teaspoon dried marjoram (substitutes oregano)"
- "1/2 cup unsweetened cashew cream or coconut cream, optional"
- "1/4 cup roasted pumpkin seeds (pepitas) for garnish"
- "Fresh parsley, chopped for garnish"
instructions:
- "Heat oil in large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat until shimmering but not smoking."
- "Add diced onions, season lightly with salt and half the pepper. Cook, stirring every minute, until onions go translucent and start to show pale golden edges, about 6-7 minutes. Soft, not browned yet. This builds a flavorful base."
- "Toss in minced garlic. Stir constantly and watch carefully. Garlic turns nutty but burns fast. Roughly 1 to 1 1/2 minutes max. Remove from direct heat if it threatens to scorch."
- "Add butternut chunks. Stir to coat in oil and aromatics. Let them sweat and heat through, about 3 minutes. They’ll start softening slightly and become fragrant."
- "Pour in broth, season again with remaining salt and pepper, add thyme, rosemary, and marjoram. Stir well. Bring liquid to a steady rolling boil—bubbles breaking surface across pot."
- "Once boiling, cover loosely, lower heat to a gentle simmer. Cook until squash gives easily to fork—around 25-35 minutes, depending on cube size. No mushy pulp, just tender softness. Stir occasionally, check aroma intensifies."
- "Remove from heat. Use immersion blender directly in pot. Blend until completely pureed with no chunks left. Stop and scrape sides to ensure even smoothness. If too thick, add small splash of broth or water."
- "If you want richness, stir in cashew cream or coconut cream now. Use unsweetened versions to avoid extra sweetness. Adds silkiness without changing flavor profile drastically. Heat gently if required, but don’t boil after cream addition."
- "Adjust seasoning: More salt to brighten, pepper for bite. Ladle into bowls. Garnish with toasted pepitas and chopped parsley for texture and freshness. A drizzle of cream on top adds visual appeal."
- "Serve hot. Good with crusty bread or simple grain bowl."
