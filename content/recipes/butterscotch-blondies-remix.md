ingredients:
- "6 tablespoons unsalted butter melted"
- "1 cup packed light brown sugar"
- "1 large egg room temperature"
- "1 teaspoon bourbon vanilla extract"
- "3/4 cup white whole wheat flour"
- "1/4 cup cake flour"
- "1/2 teaspoon baking soda"
- "1/4 teaspoon kosher salt"
- "1 teaspoon espresso powder optional"
- "3/4 cup white chocolate chunks"
- "1/4 cup toffee bits"
instructions:
- "Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Butter or line an 8x8-inch square pan with parchment paper, letting edges hang over for easy removal."
- "In a large mixing bowl, combine melted butter and packed brown sugar. Stir with a wooden spoon or spatula until shiny and no lumps. Adds sheen, integrates sugar thoroughly."
- "Crack in room-temperature egg, then stir in bourbon vanilla extract. Mix until smooth—don’t overwork. Overmix develops gluten, toughens final bite."
- "In a separate bowl, whisk white whole wheat flour, cake flour, baking soda, kosher salt, and espresso powder until fully distributed."
- "Gradually add dry to wet ingredients. Fold gently with a spatula just until no flour streaks remain. Lumps okay here—don’t beat."
- "Fold in white chocolate chunks and toffee bits. Distribute evenly. The toffee will add crisp shards, white chocolate sweetness comes in spots."
- "Pour batter into prepared pan. Spread evenly with spatula, pushing batter into corners. Surface slightly rough is fine—top will smooth out while baking."
- "Slide pan onto middle rack. Set timer loosely for 22 minutes but watch edges closely."
- "Look for edges pulling away slightly from pan sides. Surface sets but yields slightly under finger press—springy not jiggly. Light golden around edges signals done."
- "Remove from oven. Place on wire rack to cool completely—at least one hour. Cutting while warm leads to smooshy squares, crumbs fall apart."
- "Once cooled, use parchment sling to lift from pan. Cut into 3 rows by 3 columns for 9 squares. Use a sharp serrated knife, clean blade between cuts."
- "Store leftovers airtight at room temperature for 3 days. Refrigerate for up to a week but lose tenderness."
