ingredients:
- "1 cup granulated sugar"
- "3/4 cup packed light brown sugar"
- "3 large eggs at room temperature"
- "2 teaspoons vanilla extract"
- "6 tablespoons unsalted butter melted and cooled (can substitute coconut oil for dairy-free)"
- "4 ounces 70% dark chocolate melted and cooled (use bittersweet if unavailable)"
- "1 cup all-purpose flour"
- "1/2 cup Dutch-process cocoa powder sifted"
- "1/2 teaspoon baking powder"
- "1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt plus extra for sprinkling"
- "1/4 teaspoon instant espresso powder (optional but recommended)"
- "3/4 cup dark chocolate chopped into chunks (substitute semi-sweet chips if needed)"
instructions:
- "Heat oven to 350°F. Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper. Set aside."
- "Combine sugars, eggs, and vanilla in a large bowl. Whip on medium speed for 6-7 minutes until mixture thickens dramatically, pale color shows. This air incorporation creates that signature crackly top surface. Don’t skimp on this step."
- "Pour in melted butter and the melted dark chocolate. Stir or beat on medium-low just until fully blended; no chocolate streaks remain. Overbeating now will deflate your mixture and lose that fudgy texture."
- "Sift together flour, Dutch-process cocoa, baking powder, salt, and espresso powder into a bowl. Break any lumps, especially in cocoa."
- "Add dry ingredients to wet. Mix on the lowest speed just until all flour disappears. Overmixing activates gluten; cookie gets cakey or tough."
- "Fold chopped dark chocolate chunks through the batter gently. Uneven distribution is fine; chunks will settle during baking."
- "Use a 1½ tablespoon scoop to portion 12 cookies per sheet. Leave space over two sheets to avoid crowding. Dough will be sticky and dense."
- "Bake 8-13 minutes watching for crackly tops and set centers. Edges firm and centers remaining slightly puffy but not jiggly is the sweet spot. Overbaking ruins chewy interior. If unsure, poke lightly—it should spring back."
- "Rest cookies on sheet 10 minutes to firm up before moving to wire rack. Warm cookies best for sugar sprinkle—flaky sea salt adds contrast."
- "Store airtight after cooling. Reheat briefly to revive fudginess."
