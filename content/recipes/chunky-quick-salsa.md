ingredients:
- "1 clove minced garlic"
- "1 fresh serrano pepper seeded"
- "1/3 cup diced red onion"
- "1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro"
- "1 cup drained fire-roasted diced tomatoes"
- "1/4 cup canned diced green chilies"
instructions:
- "Load garlic, serrano, red onion, cilantro into the blender jar. Pulse 7-8 times until finely minced but not mushy. Listen: sharp chopping sounds, ingredients visibly broken down but separate pieces still intact."
- "Add drained fire-roasted tomatoes and green chilies next. Pulse 4-5 times or use sauce preset for 5 seconds max. Watch texture. Want chunky, not liquidy. Stop as soon as mixture thickens, small tomato bits visible but well-distributed."
- "Taste immediately, adjust salt. No fridge needed if serving ASAP. Otherwise, cover and chill to maintain vibrant freshness."
- "Use within 48 hours. Stir before serving; slight separation normal. Re-pulse briefly if too watery."
