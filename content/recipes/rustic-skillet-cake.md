ingredients:
- "1½ cups all-purpose flour"
- "½ cup whole wheat flour"
- "1 teaspoon baking powder"
- "½ teaspoon baking soda"
- "½ teaspoon salt"
- "1 teaspoon ground cinnamon"
- "¾ cup packed brown sugar"
- "2 large eggs"
- "½ cup neutral vegetable oil"
- "1 cup buttermilk"
- "1 teaspoon vanilla extract"
instructions:
- "Preheat oven around 355 degrees Fahrenheit. Grease skillet well with butter or oil and dust with flour; knocks down sticking risk."
- "In large bowl whisk dry components: flours, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, brown sugar until combined evenly."
- "Lightly beat eggs in separate bowl, add oil, buttermilk, and vanilla. Blend gently but thoroughly with fork or whisk."
- "Pour wet into dry and stir with spoon or spatula just until moistened. Batter should be slightly thick but pourable; fight urge to overmix or crumb stiffens."
- "Set batter in skillet, tap pan on counter to level and release air pockets. Texture is satiny, shiny on surface before baking."
- "Slide into oven partway down rack. After 35 minutes check with toothpick inserted near center. Should come out mostly clean but a few crumbs ok"
- "Top should be golden brown, edges pulling slightly from skillet sides; surface bounces lightly under finger touch and has a slight crust forming."
- "Turn off heat; let cake rest in pan 20 to 25 minutes. Crust firms up while inside cools a bit—too hot and inside falls apart when you invert."
- "Run butter knife around edges before turning out on wire rack to finish cooling. If sticking happens, warm skillet gently to loosen."
- "Slice with serrated knife for cleaner cuts. Holds well but still moist."
- "Store covered at room temperature for 1-2 days, refrigerate if longer. Can add a handful chopped nuts or swap spices (ginger or nutmeg) for cinnamon."
